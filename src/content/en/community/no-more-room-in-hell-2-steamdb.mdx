## What SteamDB is (and isn't)

- **It is**: an independent data tracker — prices, player charts, patch builds, Deck ratings. Nothing there is marketing.
- **It is not**: a store. You can't buy the game there — it links you to the official Steam page (or our [steam key guide](/review/no-more-room-in-hell-2-steam-key) covers the buying routes).
- **It is not**: affiliated with Valve — it's a third-party tool the community trusts for exactly that reason.
