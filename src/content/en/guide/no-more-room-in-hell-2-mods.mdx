## Does No More Room in Hell 2 have mods?

Short answer: **there's no established mod scene for No More Room in Hell 2 yet**. The game launched its 1.0 release on August 4, 2026, and mod support hasn't been a confirmed feature in the game's official communications so far. The clearest evidence point is the Steam community discussions board for the game (app 292000), where a "Mod Support?" thread exists — players asking, developers and community answering — which is the standard early signal for whether and how modding might arrive.

## Why the modding picture is quiet

A few structural reasons, drawn from the game's own design:

- **Progression integrity**: skills, gear, currency and permadeath are tied to characters on server-side runs. Client-side mods that touch inventory, health or spawns would interact directly with that economy — something a co-op game must handle carefully before opening the doors.
- **Cross-platform play**: with PC, PS5 and Xbox versions in the ecosystem, any mod support would need to work (or be explicitly restricted) across platforms, which typically delays PC-side modding.
- **Post-launch priorities**: the developer's "Beyond Armageddon" blog (August 20, 2026) lists Autumn updates focused on content, controller support, character preservation and endgame systems — mod support isn't among the announced items.

## What the community does instead

The documentation role that mods play for other games is currently filled by the **Fandom wiki** (no-more-room-in-hell-two.fandom.com), which catalogs weapons, loadouts and maps. Community guides — like Knight Knowledge's tips videos and the r/nmrih subreddit — cover the "knowledge mod" space: what to know, where to loot and how builds work.

## Where to watch for changes

- **Steam Community discussions** for app 292000 — the "Mod Support?" thread and any developer replies
- **The official dev blogs** on the studio site — post-launch announcements would precede any mod tooling
- **Nexus Mods** — worth a periodic check; if mod support ever opens, that's where it lands first
