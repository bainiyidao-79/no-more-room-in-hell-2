## The maps of No More Room in Hell 2

**No More Room in Hell 2** runs its missions across a set of large, dark, neighborhood-and-town style maps where you scavenge, complete objectives and extract. The maps confirmed across official posts and community documentation include:

- **Lewiston**
- **Pottsville**
- **Raven Rock**

## What each map plays like

From community runs and guides: **Pottsville** missions (as seen in Nightmare-difficulty solo playthroughs) chain objectives like searching houses for survivors, repairing transformers, finding fuses, tuning a radio, collecting research drives and water samples, and calling extraction from a community center. **Raven Rock** is repeatedly called out as a tight map — the kind of layout where semi-auto shotguns like the X12 Super shine. **Lewiston** is the map getting fresh objective work in the post-launch updates.

A community map tier list on r/nmrih ("I made a tier list for NMRIH 2's maps") is the live community opinion tracker, and the Fandom wiki's Maps page documents map specifics. One criticism reviewers raise: some objectives repeat within a mission (progress-bar holds, repeated searches), which is most noticeable when you learn each map's flow.

## New map content announced for Autumn 2026

Torn Banner's "Beyond Armageddon" dev blog (August 20, 2026) laid out map plans in the September update:

- **Additional objective permutations for Lewiston** — deepening the existing map with new objective flows.
- **A brand new scenario for Pottsville** — "a new objective flow that completely turns the map on its head."

The blog also noted future Survival-mode environments beyond the current Objective maps, with more to show later.

## Map tips from the community

A few map-specific lessons from the Knight Knowledge beginner guide: zeds can now climb onto cars (so car tops are a reprieve, not a safe spot), but certain objects still can't be climbed — use them to draw horde aggro while teammates handle objectives. Kicking down weak white and barricaded doors works, but blue or barred doors won't break. Press **C** for the compass pointing to your objective if you lose the thread.
