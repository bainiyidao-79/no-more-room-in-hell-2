## How skills work in No More Room in Hell 2

Skills in **No More Room in Hell 2** are tied to individual characters: as your survivors complete missions and extract successfully, they gain experience and unlock skills. Because the game uses **permadeath** (losing a character can wipe their level, skills, gear and currency on higher difficulties), the skills your character carries make each survivor genuinely meaningful — and riskier to take into Nightmare.

## What the community rates

Sportskeeda published a dedicated **"No More Room in Hell 2 Skills tier list (1.0)"** (August 2026) — the reference tier list for the current version. Community discussion also runs through r/nmrih's "Good occupations and skills?" thread, where players compare occupation choices for different team roles. Rather than re-rank every node from memory, we point you to those two sources for the full lists and keep this page focused on what's verified in multiple guides.

## The skill everyone mentions: Headblown

The one skill that appears in nearly every weapons discussion is **Headblown** for handguns. Per GameRant's guns ranking, the Headblown skill "buffs the humble 9mm round to the same level as other pistol calibers" — meaning an M9A3 pistol build with Headblown can outperform the 1911 for raw damage. That synergy is why the M9A3/MP5 9mm pairing is more viable than its stats suggest.

## Skills, permanence and the safety net

Because skills live on the character, permadeath interacts directly with your progression. Two community-relevant facts:

1. **Rescue beacons** (added in the 1.0 update) can prevent a permanently-lost character after death.
2. Torn Banner's post-launch "Beyond Armageddon" blog (August 20, 2026) confirmed work on **improving character preservation through disconnects**, plus a future **Responder Retirement** system where maxed-out characters earn boons for new Responders.
