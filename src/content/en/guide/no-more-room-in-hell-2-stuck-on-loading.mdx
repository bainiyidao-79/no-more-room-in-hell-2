**A hang on No More Room in Hell 2's loading screen almost always means damaged or missing game files.** Verifying the installation through Steam clears it in most cases; a fresh install on a different drive handles the rest. Work through the fixes in order.

## Fix 1: Verify game file integrity

1. Right-click **No More Room in Hell 2** in your Steam library and open **Properties**.
2. Go to **Installed Files** and click **Verify integrity of game files**.
3. Let Steam scan and re-download anything broken, then launch the game.

Loading hangs come from a handful of corrupted assets far more often than from hardware — this step alone resolves the majority of cases.

## Fix 2: Reinstall on a different drive

If verification reports everything fine but the game still hangs, the install location itself can be the problem — a failing sector or a drive with flaky write performance will stall streaming assets mid-load. Uninstall, then reinstall to a different drive (an SSD is strongly preferred). Players who move the installation this way report the loading hang disappearing entirely.

## Fix 3: Update your graphics driver

An outdated GPU driver can also freeze the game during the loading phase, before the first frame renders. Install the latest driver for your card from the official NVIDIA, AMD or Intel site, reboot, and try again.

## If none of it works

Check the game's official Steam discussions for same-day reports — a bad patch can cause loading stalls for everyone at once, and Torn Banner's hotfix turnaround during early access was consistently fast. Connection-related hangs that happen *after* loading, when joining friends, are a different beast: see our [server assignment failed](/guide/no-more-room-in-hell-2-server-assignment-failed) and [high ping](/guide/no-more-room-in-hell-2-high-ping) guides instead.
