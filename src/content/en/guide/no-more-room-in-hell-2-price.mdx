**No More Room in Hell 2 costs $29.99 on Steam** as a one-time purchase with no editions or DLC tiers. The price has not moved since the game first entered early access — though regional pricing means the sticker varies by country, and the title has gone on sale at least once.

## Price history at a glance

| Date | Event | Price |
|------|-------|-------|
| Oct 2024 | Early Access launch | $29.99 / ¥108, no launch discount |
| May 2025 | Themed Steam sale (zombies vs. vampires event) | ¥86.4 in China — about 20% off, a new historical low at the time |
| Sep 2026 | Full 1.0 era | $29.99, currently undiscounted |

The US price has stayed at $29.99 through early access and the 1.0 launch. Torn Banner's model is simple: one SKU, free content updates, and optional cosmetic packs added after 1.0 — the roadmap for late 2026 confirms both paid cosmetic packs and free cosmetics are coming, neither of which gates gameplay.

## Do early access buyers pay again for 1.0?

No. Every purchase made during early access automatically upgraded to the full game when version 1.0 shipped in August 2026. Torn Banner raised this point repeatedly during the early access period, and it matches how the rollout actually played out — there is no paid "next version" upgrade path.

## Where to buy

The game is sold on **Steam**, the **Epic Games Store**, **Xbox** and **PlayStation** storefronts since the 1.0 launch brought it to consoles. For current regional pricing and any live discounts, always check the [official Steam page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/292000/) — prices and sale timing are set by the store, not by us.
