**You can skip No More Room in Hell 2's opening movies by pressing the Space bar** while the intro plays. The logo stingers and cinematic cut immediately, dropping you at the main menu.

## The one exception: your first launch

The very first time you start the game, the introductory cinematic has to play through to the end before you can continue — the skip command doesn't work on that initial playthrough. Every launch after that, Space is all you need. It's a small concession: the intro doubles as the game's opening mood-setter, and it only claims your patience once.

## Worth knowing

- Skipping intros has no downside — nothing is gated behind watching them, and no settings toggle needs changing.
- If pressing Space doesn't respond, wait a beat: the skip prompt only registers once the video is actually playing, not during the initial black frame.
- Loads feel slower than they look — the movie files are cached, so subsequent launches reach the menu noticeably faster than the first.
