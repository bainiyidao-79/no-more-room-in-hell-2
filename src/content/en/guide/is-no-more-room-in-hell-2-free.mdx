**No, No More Room in Hell 2 is not free.** It is a one-time premium purchase — currently $29.99 on Steam — with no subscription, no free-to-play mode and no trial version. The confusion is understandable, though: the *original* No More Room in Hell has been free for years, and plenty of players assume the sequel follows the same model.

## Why the sequel isn't free

The first No More Room in Hell began as a community mod for Half-Life 2, built on Valve's Source engine by a scattered, part-time team, and it stayed free throughout its life. The sequel is a different beast entirely:

- **A new engine.** No More Room in Hell 2 is rebuilt on Unreal Engine 5, a far costier technology base than the Source engine of the original.
- **A full-time studio.** Torn Banner Studios — the team behind Chivalry — develops the sequel with a salaried, in-house staff rather than volunteer modders.
- **A paid, ongoing project.** Revenue from the $29.99 price funds the content pipeline: new maps, weapons and modes shipped throughout early access, with more planned after 1.0.

## What your money gets you

One purchase covers the whole game: the 8-player co-op campaign across multiple large maps, the permadeath progression system, and every content update released so far — including everything added between early access and the 1.0 launch in August 2026. Post-1.0 support adds optional cosmetic packs, but gameplay content stays free per the studio's stated plan.

If you want the free experience, the original No More Room in Hell remains available at no cost on Steam. But for the 8-player sequel, checking the [current price](/guide/no-more-room-in-hell-2-price) and the [official Steam page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/292000/) is the only way in.
