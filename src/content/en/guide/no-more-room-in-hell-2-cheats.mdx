## Why this game in particular is a bad target

**No More Room in Hell 2 is an 8-player co-op game** — unlike a solo campaign, cheats here directly affect other real players' runs. Community sentiment treats solo-queue lobbies as shared experiences; ESP and speedhacks don't just win your run, they hollow out everyone else's. And because progression (levels, skills, gear, currency) is tied to characters with permadeath on higher difficulties, a cheated run undermines the exact tension the game is built on.

Practical risks also apply to any third-party software: account penalties and malware exposure are the standard costs of running unverified executables.
