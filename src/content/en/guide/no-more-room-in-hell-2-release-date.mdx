**No More Room in Hell 2 launched into Steam Early Access on October 22, 2024, and reached its full 1.0 release on August 11, 2026.** The Torn Banner Studios co-op shooter spent almost two years in early access between those two dates — a stretch that included server struggles at launch, layoffs, a delayed roadmap and, eventually, the game's biggest update ever.

## The full release timeline

- **2021** — The sequel is announced and its Steam store page goes live, years after the original No More Room in Hell became a cult favourite.
- **October 22, 2024** — Early Access begins on Steam at $29.99. The launch build ships with the Power Plant region, 8-player co-op, permadeath, proximity voice chat and Unreal Engine 5 visuals.
- **2024–2025** — A rocky launch gives way to steady patching: hotfixes, the Reanimation update with the Broadway map, the Hospital and Beaulieu maps, and spawn-system reworks all land during early access.
- **August 2025** — Torn Banner pushes the 1.0 release window from October 2025 into the first half of 2026, citing the need for more polish. The studio also confirms the final game will not include child zombies.
- **August 11, 2026** — The 1.0 version, marketed as the **Armageddon update**, goes live simultaneously on Steam, the Epic Games Store, Xbox and PlayStation 5.
- **September 2026** — Post-launch support continues with the Resupply (1.1) update and a fresh roadmap for the rest of 2026.

## What early access originally included

The October 2024 build was a scoped-down but complete co-op loop: eight players spawned across the edges of one large Pennsylvania countryside map and had to regroup, scavenge and push toward objectives at the map's center — the Power Plant region, with a dozen-plus explorable locations hiding puzzle-like goals and rewards. Torn Banner's feature list for early access read as follows:

- 8-player online co-op with permadeath
- Character progression and stashed loadouts
- Unreal Engine 5 visuals
- Proximity VOIP plus a "party" channel for team-wide comms
- Multiple enemy types, variants and encounter scenarios
- An inventory system with melee and ranged weapons
- Trap and explosive items, plus a zombie dismemberment system

## What changed for 1.0

The Armageddon (1.0) update turned the early-access experiment into a full release: new regions and remade systems, an endgame layer, and — for the first time — console versions on Xbox Series X|S and PS5. Anyone who bought the game during early access was upgraded to the full version automatically, with no additional purchase required.
