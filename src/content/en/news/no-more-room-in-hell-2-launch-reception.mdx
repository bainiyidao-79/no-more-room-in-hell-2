**No More Room in Hell 2's early access launch turned "Mostly Negative" on Steam within 24 hours**, with the review score sitting around 30–32% in the first days. The honeymoon lasted roughly a day before the player reviews took over.

## What players said

The dominant complaint was **latency**. Co-op sessions lagged so badly that even connection-optimising tools couldn't bring ping down to playable levels, and the issue dominated early reviews. Beyond the networking, players flagged a long list of rough edges: poor game feel, frequent bugs, and disappointing server optimisation. Reviewers joked bitterly that the game felt like a shadow of expectations — some suggested it should be renamed as a sequel to another infamous zombie launch.

Amid the criticism, one feature earned near-universal praise: the unlimited-stamina sprint, which reviewers called the one design choice that clearly worked.

## What the numbers said

- **Day one (Oct 23):** reviews hovered near **30% positive** — deep into "Mostly Negative" territory.
- **By that evening:** the score sat at **32%**, with the official response already rolling out.

## The developer's same-day response

Torn Banner issued a statement on October 23 promising to collect feedback aggressively, fix critical defects, and keep shipping updates — starting with the multiplayer experience.

The technical breakdown the studio shared was candid:

- **Fast-but-bad matchmaking was pairing players across regions.** The system had been grabbing the first available players rather than waiting for same-region lobbies, sticking users with terrible routes. The fix made matching prioritise similar-ping players — and conditions improved noticeably afterwards.
- **Some cloud server instances were underpowered.** Servers in Asia and the Americas were performing at roughly half their expected capacity. The studio scheduled new instance tests for the night of October 23, planning to swap them in if they passed.

The response stabilised the situation, but the deeper reckoning came days later in the studio's launch-week reflection — see our coverage of [Torn Banner's response and hotfix plan](/news/no-more-room-in-hell-2-dev-response) for what changed next.
