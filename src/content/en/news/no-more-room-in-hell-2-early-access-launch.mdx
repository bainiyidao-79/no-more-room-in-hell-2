**No More Room in Hell 2 launched into Steam Early Access on October 22, 2024**, priced at $29.99 (¥108 in China) with no launch discount. The Torn Banner Studios co-op horror shooter arrived with online co-op, cross-platform multiplayer support and Simplified Chinese language options — the first playable version of a sequel fans had waited the better part of a decade for.

## What kind of game launched

No More Room in Hell 2 is an intense, permadeath-driven co-op survival shooter. Eight players spawn scattered across one huge map and must scavenge, regroup and survive against overwhelming zombie numbers. The pitch, straight from the studio:

- **High-intensity 8-player co-op** — eight survivors appear at different points on a giant map, forced to use wits and raw survival instinct to meet up and stay alive against unstoppable hordes. Communication and escape planning matter, because every dark corner hides danger: carve through the horde by force, or outsmart it.
- **Every encounter has more than one answer** — but permadeath means every mistake carries real consequences. The zombies react to your movement and your noise. Lure them away with a car alarm, walk them into traps, or take the louder route and blow the whole crowd.
- **Large replayable maps** — you start alone with nothing, find teammates through proximity voice, and grow the squad's arsenal from beer bottles to rifles and explosives. The standing risk: if a responder dies, the squad loses everything that character carried out of previous runs.

## Power Plant: the first region

Early access opened with a single playable region: **Power Plant**, a sprawling non-linear stretch of Pennsylvania countryside. Eight players spawn along the edges of the map and push inward through more than a dozen explorable locations — abandoned gas stations, bars, military checkpoints — each hiding puzzle-like objectives and rewards. The final goal sits at the map's center, with objectives surfacing like scenes from a classic horror movie along the way. Repairing infrastructure as you go saves more lives and improves your odds of extraction.

## The feature list at launch

The initial build included: 8-player co-op, permadeath, character progression, Unreal Engine 5 visuals, proximity VOIP plus a party comms channel, multiple enemy types and variants, unique encounter scenarios, a full inventory system, melee and ranged weapons, trap and explosive items, and a zombie dismemberment and gore system.

The launch was profitable for attention but rocky in execution — within a day, player reviews turned [decidedly sour](/news/no-more-room-in-hell-2-launch-reception). The full 1.0 version didn't arrive until August 11, 2026, after a long early access road.
