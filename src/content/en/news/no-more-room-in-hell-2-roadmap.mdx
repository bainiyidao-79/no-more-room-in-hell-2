**Torn Banner has run No More Room in Hell 2 on public roadmaps since early access** — and, unusually for the genre, has mostly delivered what it posted. Here's how the plan evolved from the first 2025 roadmap to the Autumn 2026 plan now in motion.

## The February 2025 roadmap

With the game three months into early access, Torn Banner published its update plan for spring 2025:

- **March 2025** — A gear and loadout update, expanding how players configure their responders, plus **two new weapons**.
- **April 2025** — The major **"Reanimation" update**: a brand-new map, the Combat 2.0 system rework, and sweeping quality-of-life improvements. The team confirmed more details were coming closer to release.

Both landed roughly on schedule — Reanimation brought the Broadway map and the combat rework that April, and the studio kept shipping through 2025: the Spawn Update, the Beaulieu map, the Hospital map with the 0.7.0 build, and the Health Update follow-ups.

## Then the delay — and Armageddon

The original plan had 1.0 arriving in October 2025. In August 2025 the studio [pushed that to the first half of 2026](/news/no-more-room-in-hell-2-1-0-delay) to polish difficulty balance, spawn logic and progression. The payoff arrived on **August 11, 2026**: the **Armageddon (1.0) update** launched on PC and, for the first time, Xbox and PlayStation 5.

## Resupply (1.1) and the Autumn 2026 roadmap

Post-launch support started fast. The **Resupply (1.1) update** landed September 15, 2026 with a new alternative Pottsville scenario dropping responders onto Downtown rooftops, reworked Lewiston points of interest, new firearm attachments (muzzle brakes, compensators, laser sights, shotgun chokes) and bonus-currency events.

The **Autumn 2026 roadmap**, published the next day, commits to:

- **Endgame depth** — Responder Retirement and a new Hell Mode, giving veteran players long-term goals.
- **Survival mode expansion** — two new maps for the game's newest mode, plus continued reworks of existing maps starting with Hospital.
- **New weapons** targeting under-served archetypes and ammo types.
- **UX and UI improvements**, plus new paid cosmetic packs alongside free cosmetics.
