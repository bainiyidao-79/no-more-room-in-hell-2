**In August 2025, Torn Banner delayed No More Room in Hell 2's 1.0 release from October 2025 into the first half of 2026** — and used the same announcement to rule out one of the original mod's most controversial elements for good: child zombies will not appear in the finished game.

## Why the delay

The studio's statement pointed to three inputs: the current state of development, feedback from the player community, and the team's own bar for the release. October 2025 — the one-year mark of early access — simply wasn't reachable at the quality level they wanted. "There is still plenty that needs further refinement and expansion," the statement read.

The extra time was earmarked for specific work:

- **Difficulty balance** — tuning the brutal early-game difficulty curve.
- **Enemy spawn logic** — reworking how and where the horde appears.
- **Character progression** — deepening the permadeath-driven growth systems.
- **Bug fixes and performance** — the technical debt that had shadowed the game since its rocky launch.

New content was promised alongside the polish: tighter-paced gameplay modes and **at least two brand-new maps**.

## The child zombies decision

The studio also closed a long-running community debate. Child zombies — present in the original No More Room in Hell mod and a recurring request from parts of the community — will not be in No More Room in Hell 2's final version.

The reasoning was commercial as much as creative. The team said the element posed unacceptable risks to the game's global release: exclusion from major industry showcases, blocked sales in some regions, potential platform takedowns, and player loss — "risks we cannot afford." The priority, the statement said, was the game's long-term success, and the decision was made proactively rather than under pressure.

## How it turned out

The delayed window held. Version 1.0 — the **Armageddon update** — shipped on **August 11, 2026** on Steam, Epic, Xbox and PlayStation, roughly ten months after the original target. The game's post-launch direction continues in the [Autumn 2026 roadmap](/news/no-more-room-in-hell-2-roadmap).
