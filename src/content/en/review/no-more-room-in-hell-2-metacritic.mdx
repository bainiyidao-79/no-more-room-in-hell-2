## Checking the Metacritic score

No More Room in Hell 2 has a Metacritic presence covering its **PC (Steam)** and **PS5/Xbox** releases, tracking critic and user scores as they accumulate. Because scores update as reviews land — and this site doesn't republish numbers we can't independently verify — **the Metacritic page itself is the authoritative source** for the current rating.

## What the review conversation consistently says

Across post-1.0 reviews and community verdicts (August 2026), the critical themes are stable:

**Praised:**

- **Atmosphere and tension** — lighting, fog, dark interiors and sound design built for old-school zombie horror
- **Meaningful permadeath** — character-tied progression making survival matter, softened by 1.0's rescue beacons
- **Co-op design** — where getting separated from the team is a genuine problem, in an 8-player ecosystem
- **1.0 improvements** — a major optimization round plus Steam Deck support
- **Gunplay** — solid, with balanced ammunition

**Criticized:**

- **Melee inconsistency** — hits not always connecting or registering reactions
- **Repetitive objectives** — progress-bar holds and repeated item searches within missions
- **Solo limitations** — Solo Training Mode lacks progression and permadeath

These themes come from the full review breakdown in our [review roundup](/review/no-more-room-in-hell-2-review), built on Jerry Plays' 1.0 review (239K+ views) and community verdicts.

## Context: from Early Access to 1.0

The game spent its Early Access period iterating publicly (with periodic character wipes), and the 1.0 "Armageddon" launch (August 2026) was the developer's polish milestone — including the optimization round reviewers noted. That history shapes what reviewers reward: ambition and co-op design over technical perfection.
