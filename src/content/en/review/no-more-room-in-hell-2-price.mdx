## How much does No More Room in Hell 2 cost?

At our launch-era scan (August 11, 2026, right after the 1.0 release window), **No More Room in Hell 2 was priced at $19.49 USD with a 35% launch discount** on Steam. Prices change with sales and regional pricing, so **treat the official store pages as the source of truth** for what you'll actually pay today:

- **Steam** — store.steampowered.com/app/292000/ (app ID 292000)

## Price context

A sub-$20 entry point for an 8-player co-op survival shooter puts the game on the accessible end of the genre — and the launch-window discount made the entry even easier. The game's own numbers back the value question: **1,000,000+ players since Early Access, 500,000 joining from the 1.0 launch onwards**, per the developer's "Beyond Armageddon" blog.

## What your money buys

The 1.0 "Armageddon" version includes the full co-op objective experience across Lewiston, Pottsville and Raven Rock, the Survival horde-defense mode, character progression with permadeath and rescue beacons, and the post-launch roadmap (September's map content and attachments, October gameplay items, later Responder Retirement and Hell Mode — see the [roadmap](/release/no-more-room-in-hell-2-roadmap)). Updates so far have been free content additions, not paid splits.
