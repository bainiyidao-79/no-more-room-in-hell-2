## Which platforms is No More Room in Hell 2 on?

**No More Room in Hell 2** is available on three platforms, all in the same co-op ecosystem:

- **PC via Steam** — the original platform, present since Early Access and through the 1.0 "Armageddon" launch (August 2026). Steam app ID 292000.
- **PlayStation 5** — listed on the PlayStation Store as concept 10019078; console players joined the ecosystem after the PC 1.0 launch (community threads like "No more room in hell 2 just dropped on PS5" on r/nmrih mark the moment).
- **Xbox** — available via the Microsoft Store (listing 9pf1q2f7jm9g).

## How the platforms fit together

The platforms are one ecosystem: the 1.0 launch numbers confirm it — Torn Banner's "Beyond Armageddon" blog states **500,000 players joined from the 1.0 launch onwards "on PC and consoles"**, out of over 1,000,000 total players since Early Access.

For crossplay between platforms (and its launch-period issues), see our dedicated [crossplay page](/crossplay/no-more-room-in-hell-2-crossplay). The short version: crossplay is part of the design, community threads documented problems around launch, and the September update explicitly targets "a more consistent and enjoyable experience across platforms, input methods, and skill levels."

## Platform-specific notes

- **Steam Deck**: rated "Playable" on SteamDB (tested August 13, 2026) — with notes that some in-game text is small, graphics settings need manual configuration for best performance, and all functionality is accessible with the default controller configuration.
- **Controller support**: the September update brings deadzone improvements and UI menu navigation improvements, plus hotfix access to the scoreboard and player muting on controllers — relevant for console players and PC controller users alike.
- **Progression**: skills and permadeath are tied to characters in the shared co-op economy, which is why platform parity is a stated developer priority.
