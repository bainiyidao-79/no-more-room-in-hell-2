## Is No More Room in Hell 2 crossplay?

**No More Room in Hell 2 spans PC (Steam), PS5 and Xbox**, and crossplay between these platforms is part of the game's design — the community has been actively discussing it, including its launch-period rough edges. The honest picture as of the 1.0 era:

- The game launched into 1.0 on PC first (August 2026), with **PS5 and Xbox versions arriving shortly after** — community threads like "No more room in hell 2 just dropped on PS5" on r/nmrih and review videos titled "No More Room in Hell 2 Is FINALLY On Consoles" mark the moment console players joined the same ecosystem.
- A dedicated r/nmrih thread — "No More Room In Hell 2 Crossplay Issues" — documents that **crossplay experienced problems around launch**, which is common for co-op games bridging platforms.
- Torn Banner's post-launch "Beyond Armageddon" blog (August 20, 2026) makes cross-platform experience an explicit priority: the September update targets "a more consistent and enjoyable experience across platforms, input methods, and skill levels."

## What that means for your squad

If your team is split across PC and consoles, expect the crossplay systems to be present and improving — the launch-period issues threads are the place to check before you buy, since they reflect real, current player experience rather than marketing.

## The controller side of the story

Console play and crossplay share infrastructure, so the platform-focused updates matter here too. The September update (per the dev blog) includes **controller deadzone improvements**, **UI menu navigation improvements**, and hotfix access to the **scoreboard and individual player muting** on controllers — all directly relevant to mixed-platform lobbies where you may be queued with controller players.
