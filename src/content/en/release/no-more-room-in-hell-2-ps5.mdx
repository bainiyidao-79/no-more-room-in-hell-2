## No More Room in Hell 2 on PlayStation 5

**No More Room in Hell 2 is on PlayStation 5** — the console version arrived in the 1.0 launch window (August 2026), after the PC original ran through Early Access. The game is listed on the **PlayStation Store as concept 10019078** (store.playstation.com, en-US).

## The console launch moment

Console players joined the same co-op ecosystem as PC — and the community marked it immediately. The r/nmrih thread **"No more room in hell 2 just dropped on PS5 and I had..."** captured launch impressions, while review coverage framed it as the moment the game was *"FINALLY On Consoles."* Torn Banner's own "Beyond Armageddon" blog confirmed the scale: **500,000 players joined from the 1.0 launch onwards "on PC and consoles"** (of 1,000,000+ total since Early Access).

## Controller improvements for PS5 players

The post-launch roadmap treats console input as a priority. The **September update** (per the "Beyond Armageddon" blog) delivers:

- **Controller deadzone improvements**
- **Updated and improved UI menu navigation**

And the launch-week hotfix line already added **controller access to the scoreboard and individual player muting** — the kind of lobby-management features console lobbies rely on.

## What PS5 players get

The same core game as PC: 8-player co-op objectives across Lewiston, Pottsville and Raven Rock, the Survival horde-defense mode, permadeath with rescue beacons, infection mechanics, and the full 2026 roadmap (new attachments, gameplay items, Responder Retirement and Hell Mode later in the year — see the [roadmap page](/release/no-more-room-in-hell-2-roadmap)).
