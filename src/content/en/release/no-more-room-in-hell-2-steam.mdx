## No More Room in Hell 2 on Steam

**No More Room in Hell 2** lives on Steam under **app ID 292000** (store.steampowered.com/app/292000/). Steam is the game's original platform — it carried the Early Access period through the 1.0 "Armageddon" launch in August 2026, and it's where the game posted its biggest concurrent numbers.

## Steam Deck: rated "Playable"

SteamDB documents the official Deck review (tested **August 13, 2026**, build 24479949):

- **Rating: "Playable"** — the game runs successfully on SteamOS and all functionality is accessible with the default controller configuration.
- **Caveats worth knowing**: some in-game text is small and may be difficult to read, and the game requires manual configuration of graphics settings to perform well on the Deck.
- Controller glyphs match the device, and the recommended runtime is Proton 11.0-2RC.

So yes — you can survive the apocalypse on a handheld, with settings tweaks.

## The Steam-side story so far

Steam carried the game from **Early Access** through the 1.0 "Armageddon" launch (August 2026).
