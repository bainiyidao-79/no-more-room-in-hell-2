## No More Room in Hell 2 on Xbox

**No More Room in Hell 2 is available on Xbox** via the Microsoft Store (listing 9pf1q2f7jm9g on xbox.com). The Xbox version joined the ecosystem in the **1.0 launch window of August 2026**, alongside the PS5 rollout — part of the console expansion that took the game from PC Early Access to a three-platform co-op ecosystem.

## Scale of the console rollout

Torn Banner's "Beyond Armageddon" blog (August 20, 2026) put numbers on it: **500,000 players joined from the 1.0 launch onwards "on PC and consoles"**, out of over 1,000,000 total players since Early Access. The concurrent peak of **16,330 players (August 23, 2026)** came right after that console rollout — see the [player count page](/crossplay/no-more-room-in-hell-2-player-count).

## Controller improvements for Xbox players

The post-launch roadmap treats controller input as a standing priority, per the "Beyond Armageddon" blog:

- **September update**: controller **deadzone improvements** plus **updated and improved UI menu navigation**.
- **Launch hotfixes**: controller access to the **scoreboard and individual player muting**.

These land alongside the platform-wide quality-of-life goal: "a more consistent and enjoyable experience across platforms, input methods, and skill levels."

## What Xbox players get

The same core game as PC and PS5 — 8-player co-op objectives across Lewiston, Pottsville and Raven Rock, the Survival horde-defense mode, permadeath with rescue beacons, infection mechanics (pills and gene therapy), and the full 2026 roadmap including **Hell Mode** before year's end ([roadmap page](/release/no-more-room-in-hell-2-roadmap)).

## Crossplay on Xbox

Xbox players share the co-op ecosystem with PC and PS5 — see our [crossplay page](/crossplay/no-more-room-in-hell-2-crossplay) for the platform-bridging status and launch-period issues the community has reported.
