## The official post-launch roadmap

Torn Banner laid out the future of **No More Room in Hell 2** in the **"Beyond Armageddon" developer blog (August 20, 2026)** on the official site (nmrih2.com) — the authoritative roadmap source. The headline context: the game has passed **1,000,000 players since Early Access**, with **500,000 joining from the 1.0 launch onwards on PC and consoles**, and **multiple updates are planned for 2026 — two of them slated for Autumn**.

## The September update

- **New map content**: additional **objective permutations for Lewiston**, plus a **brand new scenario for Pottsville** — described as "a new objective flow that completely turns the map on its head."
- **New weapon attachments**: from muzzle modifications to laser sights, "that meaningfully adjust what your firearms can accomplish."
- **Controller support**: deadzone improvements plus UI menu navigation improvements — the post-launch quality-of-life push for "a more consistent and enjoyable experience across platforms, input methods, and skill levels."
- Hotfix work alongside it: controller access to the scoreboard and individual player muting.

## The October update

More **gameplay items** that "slowly offer more choices during player's runs without overwhelming new Responders or invalidating past builds," with content previews continuing as they did on the Road to 1.0.

## Endgame systems (starting later in Autumn)

- **Responder Retirement** — a new system letting players send maxed-out characters to rest, in exchange for major gameplay boons benefiting future Responders and exclusive cosmetic items. Built for players who maxed their camp and beat every map on the highest difficulties.
- **Responder Memorial** — a way to look back on past Responders, "whether they are growing old with civilians or perished in the field," available to all players.
- **Improved XP events** — with in-game indicators showing when boosted levels or currency are active.
- **Solo Mode expansion** — under investigation ("beyond a match by match basis") while keeping the co-op focus; see our [solo page](/crossplay/no-more-room-in-hell-2-solo).

## Hell Mode — before the end of 2026

The blog's long-teased reveal: **"Hell Mode"**, an experience beyond Nightmare — difficult, endgame-focused, asking players to "bring their best Responders and invest everything for a *chance* at success." Torn Banner states it's hoping to release **before the end of 2026**, with details to come.

## Also worth knowing

Launch gratitude came with perks: **3 Merits were distributed to every player** who logged in before August 24, 2026 (12:00 EDT), and work continues on **improving character preservation through disconnects** — released "in waves as they are ready."
